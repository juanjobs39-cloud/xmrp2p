## Is it legal to own Monero in Mexico?

- **Owning and buying:** no law forbids an individual from owning or buying Monero.
- **Banks and fintechs:** Banxico's Circular 4/2019 limits what regulated institutions can do with virtual assets for their clients. That is why your bank does not sell you crypto, but you can still send a SPEI to another person.
- **Selling as a business:** habitual, professional exchange of virtual assets is a **vulnerable activity** (article 17, section XVI of the LFPIORPI, since 2019), with registration and reports to the SAT.
- **Taxes:** gains when selling may be subject to income tax (ISR).

This is general information, not legal or tax advice. If you trade often, ask an accountant.

## How to avoid scams when buying Monero in Mexico

- **Never pay before the XMR is in escrow.** Anyone asking you to pay first is a scammer.
- **Pay only to the CLABE shown in the order**, from an account in your own name. Third-party payments are used in **triangulation** scams, where a victim of another fraud is made to pay you.
- **Do not close deals on Telegram or WhatsApp.** Keep everything inside the order so the arbiter has the evidence.
- **Keep your SPEI receipt** with its tracking key until the XMR arrives.
- **Use your own wallet**, not an exchange's: [best Monero wallets in 2026](/en/news/best-monero-wallets-2026/).
