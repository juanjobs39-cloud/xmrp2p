**In short:** MoneroP2P now lets you **swap Bitcoin for Monero (BTC → XMR)** and the other way round. You type how much BTC you send and the app shows quotes from several instant exchanges, sorted by how much XMR you get, with each exchange's KYC rating and estimated time. No account, no sign-up.

[Swap BTC for XMR now →](/en/app/?coin=btc%7CMainnet)

## How to swap Bitcoin for Monero, step by step

1. Open the [app](/en/app/?coin=btc%7CMainnet) and, in **You send**, choose **BTC · Bitcoin** (or **BTC · Lightning** for faster, cheaper payments).
2. Type the amount of BTC and press **Exchange**.
3. Compare the quotes: XMR you get, exchange, KYC rating, fixed or floating rate, and estimated time.
4. Press **Choose** and enter your **Monero address**. Optionally add a Bitcoin refund address.
5. Send the **exact amount of BTC** to the deposit address shown. The status updates on its own every minute.
6. When the exchange finishes, the XMR arrives in your wallet.

Example from October 30, 2026: **0.001 BTC → about 0.128 XMR**, with 19 exchanges quoting. Prices change all the time; the app always shows the current quote before you send anything.

## Monero to Bitcoin too (XMR → BTC)

Press the **⇅** button between "You send" and "You get" to **sell XMR**: choose BTC, type how much XMR you send and enter your Bitcoin address. [Swap XMR for BTC →](/en/app/?side=sell&coin=btc%7CMainnet)

## What you should know

| | BTC ↔ XMR on MoneroP2P |
|---|---|
| Who does the swap | An instant exchange you choose, via Trocador |
| Custody | The exchange holds the funds during the swap |
| Account / ID | No account; only exchanges rated A or B |
| Time | Usually 10–60 min (Bitcoin confirmations) |
| Minimum | Depends on the exchange, usually around 0.0001 BTC |
| Data we keep | When you create a swap: IP, language and browser, as the exchanges require ([privacy](/en/privacy/)) |

**Want no custody at all?** MoneroP2P's own atomic swaps are for **USDT on Arbitrum One**: nobody holds your funds and a contract refunds you if something fails. [How it works](/en/how-it-works/). For BTC ↔ XMR without custody there are also atomic-swap projects such as eigenwallet.

## Tips

- Use your own Monero wallet. [How to create one](/en/guides/how-to-create-a-monero-wallet/).
- Check that you send **Bitcoin on the Bitcoin network** (or Lightning if you chose Lightning).
- Keep the swap ID: Trocador support can help with it if something goes wrong.
- Compare by **XMR received**, not by advertised fees. [How much a swap really costs](/en/guides/how-much-does-it-cost-to-swap-usdt-for-monero/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I swap Bitcoin for Monero?
Open the app, choose BTC in "You send", type the amount and press Exchange. Pick a quote, enter your Monero address and send the exact BTC amount to the deposit address shown.

### Do I need an account or KYC?
No. There are no accounts. The app only shows exchanges rated A or B by Trocador, which do not ask for ID in normal trades; an exchange may still hold a trade it flags as high risk.

### How long does it take?
Usually 10 to 60 minutes. Each quote shows the estimated time; Bitcoin needs confirmations first. Lightning is usually faster.

### Is it non-custodial?
Not for BTC: the exchange holds the funds during the swap. MoneroP2P's non-custodial atomic swaps are for USDT on Arbitrum One.

### Can I swap Monero back to Bitcoin?
Yes, with the ⇅ button in the app, or directly at [monerop2p.app/en/app/?side=sell](/en/app/?side=sell&coin=btc%7CMainnet).

Before using any exchange, read the [risk notice](/en/risk-notice/).
