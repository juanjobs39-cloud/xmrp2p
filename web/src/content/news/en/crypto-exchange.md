## How does the exchange work?

MoneroP2P does not make up prices: it asks the instant exchanges aggregated by [Trocador](https://trocador.app/) for quotes and shows them in one list.

1. Under **You send**, pick the coin and its network. Popular ones come first; the search covers 2,500+.
2. Enter **any amount**. There is no cap to stay under anymore.
3. Each quote shows what matters: **XMR you get**, exchange, **KYC rating**, **fixed or floating** rate and **estimated time**. The best one is highlighted.
4. Pick one, paste your **Monero address** and send the exact amount. The status refreshes by itself, and if you reload the page the exchange comes back.

## Is there an amount limit?

**Not on the exchange.** Enter any amount and each exchange shows whether it accepts it; each has its own **minimum**, and if your amount is too small no quotes appear.

The only cap belongs to the **non-custodial atomic swap** for USDT on Arbitrum One: **100 USDT per swap**, set in its contract. If you pick Arbitrum USDT and makers cover your amount, the app offers that swap first; otherwise it shows the instant exchanges without you doing anything.

## How much does it cost?

- **Everything is inside the quote:** the exchange's margin and a **1% margin for MoneroP2P**. No separate charges.
- **Your network fee:** your wallet pays it when sending the deposit, like any transfer.
- **USDT atomic swap on Arbitrum:** 0.5% paid by the maker; you only pay gas.

## Is it safe?

- **Custody during the swap.** Instant exchanges hold your coins for a few minutes and then send the XMR. For **zero custody**, use the [atomic swap](/en/guides/what-is-an-atomic-swap/) for USDT on Arbitrum One: a contract refunds your USDT if something fails.
- **Check the network.** Sending USDT over TRC20 to an address that expects ERC20 can lose the funds. [Common mistakes when sending crypto](/en/guides/common-mistakes-when-sending-crypto/).
- **Keep the exchange ID.** Trocador support can help you with it if something gets stuck.
- **Use your own Monero wallet.** [Best Monero wallets in 2026](/en/news/best-monero-wallets-2026/).
- **Data:** when you create an exchange via Trocador we keep IP, language and browser linked to its ID, as those exchanges require. Details in [privacy](/en/privacy/).
