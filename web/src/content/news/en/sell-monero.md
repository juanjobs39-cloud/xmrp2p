## How to sell Monero for USDT, step by step

1. Open the [app](/en/app/?side=sell&coin=usdt%7CTRC20) and press **⇅** between "You send" and "You get" to sell XMR.
2. Under **You get**, choose the coin and network, for example **USDT · Tron (TRC20)**.
3. Enter how much **XMR** you sell and press **Exchange**.
4. Compare: what you get, the exchange, its KYC rating, fixed or floating rate and estimated time. Press **Choose** on the best one.
5. Paste your **USDT address on that same network** (TRC20 addresses start with **T**) and, if you like, your Monero address for a refund.
6. Send the **exact amount of XMR** to the deposit address. The status updates by itself and tells you when the deposit is received and waiting for confirmations.
7. After about 10 Monero confirmations (about 20 minutes), the exchange sends you the USDT.

Closed the page? Go back to the app: the swap resumes by itself for 24 hours, or with its ID.
