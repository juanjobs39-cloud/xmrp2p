## Which method is best?

- **Instant and non-reversible:** SPEI, Pix, Nequi, Yape, SEPA Instant, Faster Payments, Interac, UPI or Zelle. The money arrives in seconds and there are no chargebacks.
- **Reversible:** PayPal, Revolut, Mercado Pago or Cash App. The platform flags them because the payment can be reversed after release; many sellers ask for history.
- **Cash in person:** for small amounts, in a public place.

## How does buying work?

1. Open the [market](https://fiat.monerop2p.app/en/app/), pick your currency and create an account with a nickname and password.
2. Pick an ad with an instant method and type how much you want to spend.
3. The seller locks the XMR in the **2-of-3 escrow** (buyer, seller and arbiter). **You do not pay until the app confirms the deposit.**
4. Pay from **an account in your own name** and tap “I have paid”.
5. The seller releases and the XMR reaches your wallet. If they do not, open a dispute and the arbiter decides; it can only send the XMR to the buyer or back to the seller.

## Is buying Monero legal?

It depends on the country, and this is not legal advice:

- **Mexico:** individuals can own it; anyone exchanging it as a business has anti-money-laundering duties. Details in [buy Monero in Mexico](/en/news/where-to-buy-monero-in-mexico/).
- **European Union:** under the new anti-money-laundering regulation, from July 2027 regulated companies will not be allowed to handle privacy coins. The law does not forbid individuals from owning them or buying them person to person.
- **Other countries:** check local rules and taxes on gains.

## How to avoid scams

- **Never pay before the XMR is in escrow.**
- **Pay only to the details shown in the order**, from an account in your own name. Third-party payments are used in triangulation scams with stolen money.
- **Do not close deals on Telegram or WhatsApp**: keep everything inside the order.
- **Use your own wallet:** [best Monero wallets in 2026](/en/news/best-monero-wallets-2026/).
