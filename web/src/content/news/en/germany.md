## Why can't you buy Monero on exchanges in Germany anymore?

- **Binance** delisted Monero worldwide in **February 2024**.
- **Kraken** ended XMR trading for customers in the **European Economic Area** on **31 October 2024**, citing regulatory changes.
- The **EU anti-money-laundering regulation (AMLR)** bars regulated crypto service providers from offering anonymity-enhancing coins from **July 2027**. So no German or European KYC exchange is going to relist Monero.

Monero itself is not affected: the network keeps running and **self-custody wallets stay legal**. Only *where* you get it changes. More in [Monero delisted: where to buy XMR in 2026](/en/news/monero-delisted-where-to-buy-xmr-in-2026/).

## Is Monero legal in Germany?

**Yes.** No German or EU law forbids individuals from buying, holding, sending or receiving Monero.

| Rule | What it covers | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| **MiCA** (since December 2024) | Crypto service providers in the EU | Exchanges need a licence; many dropped XMR because of it |
| **Transfer of Funds Regulation** (since December 2024) | Transfers between exchanges and to self-custody wallets | Your exchange may ask you to prove you own the wallet |
| **AMLR** (from July 2027) | Regulated providers | They may no longer offer privacy coins; **self-custody wallets are not affected** |

This is not legal advice. Follow the law and ask a lawyer if in doubt.

## Do I pay tax on Monero in Germany?

In Germany, crypto held privately falls under the rules for **private sales (§ 23 EStG)**:

- **Held for more than one year:** gains on selling or swapping are **tax-free**.
- **Within one year:** gains are taxed at your personal rate once all private sale gains for the year reach the **1,000 euro exemption limit** (Freigrenze).
- **Swaps count:** swapping Bitcoin for Monero is a sale of the Bitcoin for tax purposes. Write down date, amount and price.

Ask a tax adviser if you move larger amounts.
