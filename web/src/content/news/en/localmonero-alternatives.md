## What happened to LocalMonero

| Date | What happened |
|---|---|
| May 7, 2024 | LocalMonero and AgoraDesk announce they are closing after almost 7 years. |
| May 14, 2024 | They stop accepting new trades, signups and ads. |
| November 7, 2024 | Final shutdown. Arbitration ran until that day. |

LocalMonero was **the most used P2P market for Monero**: it connected buyers and sellers who paid by bank transfer, cash or payment apps, with an escrow held by the platform itself. Its closure coincided with a wave of **exchanges delisting Monero**, so buying XMR with local currency became much harder. ## MoneroP2P Fiat: the closest alternative to LocalMonero

It works the way you remember: you find an ad in your currency, open an order, pay the seller and get your XMR. The difference is who holds the Monero in the meantime.

- **2-of-3 multisig escrow.** The seller's XMR is locked on the Monero network before you pay. Moving it takes 2 of 3 signatures (buyer, seller and arbitrator), so **neither the platform nor any single person can take it**.
- **The chat and payment details open after the deposit.** If the seller doesn't deposit within 30 minutes, the order is cancelled and you haven't paid anything.
- **No KYC or email.** Your account is a key generated by your browser, protected with a password.
- **Clear fees.** Buying is free. The seller pays **0.5%** on release, plus the network fee.
- **Limits.** Up to 1,000 USD (or equivalent) per order and 3 open orders per user.
- **Live ads.** If an advertiser has been offline for more than 24 hours, their ads are hidden automatically, so you don't waste time on abandoned accounts.
- **Appeals.** If you paid and the seller doesn't release, you open an appeal after 15 minutes; support reviews the proof and signs with whoever is right.

## How to buy and sell XMR P2P safely

1. **Never pay before the order says the XMR is in escrow.**
2. **Don't leave the platform.** Anyone asking you to continue on WhatsApp or Telegram, or to pay outside the order, removes the escrow's protection. It's the most common scam.
3. **Pay from an account in your name** and keep the receipt.
4. **Receive into your own wallet.** If you don't have one yet, see the [best Monero wallets in 2026](/en/news/best-monero-wallets-2026/).
5. **Start with a small amount** with a new seller.
