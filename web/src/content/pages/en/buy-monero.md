**Short answer:** to **buy Monero (XMR) without KYC** on MoneroP2P you have three paths. If you pay with **dollars, euros or another local currency**, use the [P2P Fiat](https://fiat.monerop2p.app/en/) market. If you hold **USDT**, use the [non-custodial atomic swap](/en/app/). If you hold **BTC, ETH or other crypto**, compare instant-exchange quotes in the same [app](/en/app/). None of them asks for an ID-verified account.

## 1. Buy Monero P2P with local currency

The option for people with money in the bank or in cash and no crypto yet. It works like LocalMonero, which shut down in 2024, except the platform never holds the XMR:

1. Open [P2P Fiat](https://fiat.monerop2p.app/en/app/) and pick your currency and payment method.
2. Take an ad from an online seller. The seller deposits the XMR into a **2-of-3 multisig escrow**.
3. After 10 confirmations (~20 min) the chat and payment details unlock. Pay and mark "I have paid".
4. The seller confirms and the XMR lands in your wallet. If there is a problem, support settles the appeal using the proofs.

Guides by method: [Zelle](https://fiat.monerop2p.app/en/buy-monero-with-zelle/), [SEPA](https://fiat.monerop2p.app/en/buy-monero-with-sepa/), [Wise](https://fiat.monerop2p.app/en/buy-monero-with-wise/), [Revolut](https://fiat.monerop2p.app/en/buy-monero-with-revolut/), [Interac](https://fiat.monerop2p.app/en/buy-monero-with-interac/), [UPI](https://fiat.monerop2p.app/en/buy-monero-with-upi/), [cash](https://fiat.monerop2p.app/en/buy-monero-with-cash/). By country: [the United States](https://fiat.monerop2p.app/en/buy-monero-in-the-united-states/), [Spain](https://fiat.monerop2p.app/en/buy-monero-in-spain/), [Mexico](https://fiat.monerop2p.app/en/buy-monero-in-mexico/), [Brazil](https://fiat.monerop2p.app/en/buy-monero-in-brazil/), [and more](https://fiat.monerop2p.app/en/countries/).

## 2. Swap USDT for Monero without intermediaries

If you already hold USDT on **Arbitrum One**, the [atomic swap](/en/how-it-works/) is the non-custodial option: your USDT sits in a contract that only pays the maker once you can take the XMR. If anything fails, the contract refunds you. Up to 100 USDT per swap. More in [how to swap USDT for Monero](/en/guides/how-to-swap-usdt-for-monero-without-intermediaries/).

## 3. Swap Bitcoin or other crypto for Monero

With BTC, ETH, LTC, SOL or any of 2,500+ coins, the [app](/en/app/?coin=btc%7CMainnet) shows instant-exchange quotes sorted by how much XMR you get, only from exchanges rated KYC A or B. Here the exchange does hold your funds during the swap (usually 10 to 60 minutes).

## Tips to buy XMR safely

- **Use your own wallet** ([how to create one](/en/guides/how-to-create-a-monero-wallet/)) and never leave XMR on an exchange.
- **On P2P, never pay before** the XMR is in escrow. Anyone asking you to pay off-platform is a scammer.
- **Check the address** before sending: crypto transactions cannot be reversed. See [common mistakes](/en/guides/common-mistakes-when-sending-crypto/).
- Start with a small amount the first time.

## FAQ

### Where can I buy Monero without KYC?
On MoneroP2P: P2P Fiat with local currency, the non-custodial USDT swap or instant exchanges rated KYC A or B.

### How do I buy Monero with dollars, euros or cash?
With [P2P Fiat](https://fiat.monerop2p.app/en/): you pay by bank transfer, Zelle, SEPA, Wise, Revolut or cash once the seller's XMR is in escrow.

### What is the cheapest way?
If you hold USDT on Arbitrum, the atomic swap (0.5% paid by the maker, plus gas). On P2P Fiat buying is free; each seller sets the price.

### What happened to LocalMonero?
It shut down in 2024. P2P Fiat covers the same use, with the XMR in a 2-of-3 multisig instead of held by the platform.

### Do I need a Monero wallet?
Yes, the XMR goes to your own address. You can create one for free with Cake Wallet, Feather or the official wallet.

### Are there limits?
P2P Fiat: 1000 USD per order. USDT swap: 100 USDT per swap. Exchanges have their own minimums.
