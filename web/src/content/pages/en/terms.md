## The service

MoneroP2P provides a web interface and open-source software to perform atomic swaps between USDT and Monero with independent counterparties (makers). We are not a party to the trade, we do not hold funds and we cannot reverse transactions.

## Testing status

The service runs with real money on Arbitrum One (USDT) and Monero, with a maximum of 100 USDT per swap enforced by the contract. A practice mode on test networks (testnet) is also available; test tokens have no value. The contract has been verified with automated tools (Halmos, Aderyn, Slither) but has not had an external audit; you use the service at your own risk.

## Your responsibilities

- Keep your recovery file and its password safe.
- Check the addresses you enter.
- Act within the contract deadlines (or keep the app open so it can).
- Comply with your country's laws, including tax and currency-control rules.
- Not use the service for unlawful activities.

## Fees

The contract charges a percentage fee on each completed swap, capped at 1% in the code. Networks charge their own fees too (gas on the USDT network and the Monero network fee).
